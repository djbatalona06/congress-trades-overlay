# Platform terms review

A worksheet for the owner (and a lawyer) to check each supported site's terms against what Capitol Tape actually does. **Not legal advice.** Fill one table per platform, quote the clause, and record the decision.

## What Capitol Tape does on these pages

- Reads the ticker symbol from the page **address or title**. Nothing else on the page is read.
- Draws its own panel, edge tab and signal box on top of the page (in a shadow root, so the page's own content is untouched).
- Does **not** click, type, submit, log in, read prices or account data, call the platform's APIs, or automate trading.
- Store screenshots currently show TradingView's interface and a company logo (NVIDIA). These need permission or replacement.

## Questions to answer for each platform

1. Do the terms forbid browser extensions or "overlays" on the site?
2. Do they forbid "automated means", "scraping" or "data mining"? Does reading the URL/title count?
3. Do they restrict use of their market data (for example display-only or non-display rules)? Capitol Tape does not use their data, but say so.
4. Do they restrict using their name or logo, and screenshots of their interface, in marketing or store listings?
5. Is there an official partner or integration programme that would be a safer route?
