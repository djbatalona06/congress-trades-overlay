Changelog

Newest first. Each entry says what changed in plain words.

Version 1.1.0

October 2026

  • A slim tab on the edge of the chart replaces the box. It shows the ticker and a count; open it for the full list.
  • A Pop out button opens the trades in their own window, handy on a second screen. The window follows the chart you are looking at and remembers its size and place.
  • The popup has three tabs (Watchlist, Plan, Lookups) and clearer messages about your plan.
  • Easier-to-read colours, larger buttons, and respect for the "reduce motion" setting.
  • A short "what's new" card appears after an update, so the data never changes under you without notice.

Version 1.0.0

October 2026

  • First version: congressional trade disclosures on TradingView, Robinhood, Webull and Schwab.
  • Corporate-bond charts on TradingView map to the issuer's stock.
  • Free watchlist of 3 tickers. Pro ($8/month) adds an unlimited watchlist and a browser notification when a watched ticker gets a new filing.

Coming next

Planned, not promised: a graph view beside the trade list in the pop-out window, and a summary view with date ranges. Nothing on this page counts anything about you.
