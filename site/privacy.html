Privacy Policy — Capitol Tape

Last updated: October 17, 2026

What the extension sends, and to whom

  • Ticker symbols, to Bargo. When you view a supported stock page, the extension reads the ticker symbol from the page address or title and sends that symbol to the Bargo Congress Trades API (www.bargo.ai) to retrieve public STOCK Act disclosures. Tickers on your watchlist are sent the same way when the extension checks for new disclosures. Bargo receives the request from your IP address, as any website you visit does. If you enter your own Bargo API key, it is sent with these requests.
  • Payment status, to ExtensionPay. The extension asks ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) whether this installation has a paid subscription. If you upgrade, payment is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe on their pages; the extension never sees your card details.

Nothing else leaves your browser. The extension has no server of its own and no analytics. It does not need an account.

What stays on your device

  • Your watchlist, recent lookup results, the daily lookup count, your panel preferences and, if you add one, your Bargo API key are stored in the browser's extension storage.
  • ExtensionPay stores an anonymous installation key, which your browser may sync across your signed-in devices.

If you create an account on this website

An account is optional. You only need one if you want to sign in on this website; the extension works without it. When you choose Continue with Google or Continue with GitHub, that service tells us three things: your name, your email address, and a private ID it gives you. We never see your Google or GitHub password.

  • We store that name, email address, ID, and which service you used, so we can recognise you next time.
  • We set one cookie to keep you signed in for up to 30 days. It is used for nothing else. Signing out removes it.
  • We do not sell this information, show ads with it, or share it with data brokers.
  • To have your account and its data deleted, write to the contact address below.

What the extension does not do

It does not read account balances, positions, orders or any other brokerage data, does not place trades, and does not sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Not financial advice

The extension displays publicly available STOCK Act data for information only. It is not financial, investment or trading advice. Data may be delayed or incomplete; verify with official sources.
