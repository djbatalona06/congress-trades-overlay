Security & Permissions

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Short version: Capitol Tape reads the ticker symbol from the page address or title. It cannot see your brokerage account, balances, positions or orders, and it cannot place trades.

Permissions the extension asks for

Extension permissions and why each is needed
PermissionWhy
storageKeeps your watchlist, recent results, the daily lookup count, panel settings and your optional Bargo key on your device.
alarmsRuns the hourly watchlist check (Pro) and clears old saved results.
notificationsTells you when a watched ticker gets a new filing or turns Hot (Pro).
Access to bargo.aiFetches public disclosure data for the ticker on screen.
Runs on TradingView, Robinhood, Webull and Schwab pagesReads the ticker from the page address or title and draws the panel. No other page content is read.
Firefox only: access to extensionpay.comLets the payment library check whether you have Pro.

It does not ask for your browsing history, cookies, or access to any other website.

What leaves your browser

  • The ticker symbol you are viewing, and the tickers on your watchlist, sent to Bargo to get filings.
  • A check with ExtensionPay of whether this installation has Pro.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking. All code ships inside the extension package; it loads no remote code.

Payments

Pro is bought through ExtensionPay and Stripe on their own pages. Capitol Tape never sees or stores card details.

This website

  • Served over HTTPS only, with security headers that block framing, content sniffing and scripts from other sites.
  • The optional sign-in uses Google or GitHub. We never see your password. One cookie keeps you signed in; it is secure and not readable by scripts.
