Newest first. The extension also shows a short note the first time you open it after an update.

Extension 1.2.0

  • Everything is free: a watchlist with no limit and a notification when a watched stock gets a new filing or turns Hot.
  • Every trade has a Search official records link that opens the House or Senate site where the filing is kept.
  • No requests for money next to the trades. Pro is paused; past payers can still open Manage subscription in the Plan tab.
  • Policy Pro, about bills and executive orders, is coming. Join the waitlist on the Extension page.

Website — October 2026

  • New calm, neutral look. Colour is used only for the Quiet, Watch and Hot levels, and each has its own icon.
  • New pages: Terms of Service, Data & Disclaimers, Security & Permissions, FAQ and this changelog.
  • Step-by-step help for adding a free Bargo key.

Extension 1.1.0

  • A small tab on the edge of the chart replaces the box, so the chart stays clear.
  • Pop out: open the trades in their own window, for a second screen.
  • Floating signal box: Quiet, Watch or Hot, with tags that explain why. Free, and it uses no extra lookups.
  • Pro: a notification when a stock you watch turns Hot.
  • The popup has three tabs and clearer plan messages, including a visible message when a payment check fails.
  • Easier-to-read colours, larger buttons, full keyboard use and reduced-motion support.
  • The daily lookup limit is explained in plain words, with a link to get a free key.

Extension 1.0.0

  • First version: congressional trade disclosures on TradingView, Robinhood, Webull and Schwab.
  • Corporate bond charts on TradingView show the issuing company's disclosures.
  • A small free watchlist; Pro added an unlimited watchlist and new-filing notifications.
