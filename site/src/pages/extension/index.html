How it works

Three steps to your first disclosure.

  1. Add it

    Add Capitol Tape to desktop Chrome or Firefox. No sign-up.

  2. Open a stock

    Open a stock on TradingView, Robinhood, Webull or Schwab. The symbol is read from the page.

  3. Open the tab

    Click the small tab on the right edge of the chart to see who in Congress traded it, and when. Press Esc to close it.

FAQ

Questions about the extension.

Is everything really free?

Yes. The panel, the signal box, the watchlist, new-filing notifications and the official record links are free for everyone. The trade data will stay free.

Can it see my brokerage account?

No. It reads only the ticker symbol from the page address or title. It cannot read balances, positions or orders, and it cannot place trades. See every permission on the Security page.

I paid for Pro before. What now?

Everything you had is now free. To manage an old subscription, open the extension popup, go to the Plan tab and click Manage subscription.

Is this financial advice?

No. Capitol Tape displays public STOCK Act disclosures for information only. Filings can be up to 45 days late; check the official House and Senate records.
