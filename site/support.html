Support

Quick answers first. Contact details are at the bottom.

The tab or panel does not show up

It stays hidden when there is nothing to show: the page is not a stock page on TradingView, Robinhood, Webull or Schwab, the ticker was not recognised, no member filed a disclosure in the last ~90 days, or you dismissed it. Reload the page and try another ticker such as NVDA to check.

It says I reached the daily limit

The free data source allows a set number of lookups per day. Results are saved on your device for 6 hours, and half of the day's lookups are kept for charts. A free Bargo key (opens in a new tab) pasted into the popup (Lookups tab) raises the limit. The count resets later in the day.

How do I open the second-screen window?

Open the edge tab on the chart, then choose the Pop out button. Move the window to your other screen. It follows whichever chart tab you are looking at.

How do I upgrade or cancel Pro?

Open the extension popup, then the Plan tab. Choose Upgrade to Pro, or Manage subscription to cancel. Payment is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe, so the extension never sees your card.

Pro shows as "offline" or "could not check"

The extension could not reach the payment service. Your Pro features keep working for up to 72 hours while it retries. Check your internet connection, then reopen the popup.

Are the trades up to date?

No disclosure is instant. Members have up to 45 days after a trade to file, so every row is delayed by law. Each row shows both the trade date and the filing date.
