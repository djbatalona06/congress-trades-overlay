1. What Capitol Tape is

Capitol Tape is a browser extension and website that display publicly available U.S. congressional stock trade disclosures (STOCK Act data) next to the charts you already view. It is read-only: it cannot see your brokerage account or place trades.

2. Not financial advice

Nothing here is financial, investment, legal or tax advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell anything. A member of Congress trading a stock says nothing reliable about how that stock will perform. Disclosures can be late, incomplete or wrong. Check official sources before you rely on them.

3. How you may use it

  • Use it for your own information. Do not use disclosure data to harass or threaten anyone, or for any purpose the law prohibits.
  • Do not copy, resell or republish the data it shows in bulk. The data comes from a third party (Bargo) and is subject to its own terms.
  • Do not try to break, overload or reverse-engineer the service.
